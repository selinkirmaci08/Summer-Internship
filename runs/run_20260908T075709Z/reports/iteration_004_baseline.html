Why windows diverge across horizons: At h=1–30, a narrow w=4 window (the last 4 same-DOW observations) adapts quickly to the post-regime level and excludes stale pre-COVID observations. Why training rows, not validation rows?

Part B selects feature columns that will be used by the ML model during training. Scoring on training rows reflects the feature's usefulness to the model — not its standalone predictive accuracy. Scoring on validation rows would introduce leakage: the model could implicitly select features that happen to be good on the val set.

De-duplication rationale

smedian_8 is consistently dropped at h=1–14 because it correlates r>0.98 with smedian_13. Keeping near-identical columns adds noise to tree-split decisions without new information. At h=60–90, multiple candidates cluster near smedian_26 and are similarly pruned.

Methodology
Seasonal Parameters & Protocol

Methodology and Key Rules

Seasonal Parameters

P = 7 (weekly period)
S = 52 same-DOW slots/year
ACF source: EDA lag-6 = 0.582

P=7 is set from the EDA dominant ACF lag (6, pre-regime weekly). S=52 means one seasonal year equals 52 same-DOW season-slots. A window of w=52 therefore corresponds to exactly one year of same-DOW observations — the widest seasonally-meaningful window. smedian_w52 performs second-best at h=90 in fold 3 (42.105) before pooling, confirming the annual cycle is present.

Zero Handling

Zero-demand days (50 over 10 years — public holidays and voluntary closures) are included as valid observations in same-DOW slot statistics. They reflect true shop-level demand and must not be imputed or excluded: a model that learns the season must also learn that some same-DOW slots have zero demand.

Trailing mean/median (tmean, tmed) and damped drift (ddrift) consistently underperform seasonal median by 5–30 units. They do not capture weekly seasonality and are not carried forward as feature candidates.

Fold Priority

Part A primary evaluation uses fold 3 validation rows (most recent, most representative of the 2021+ holdout period). Folds 1 and 2 are used only for h=90, where fold 3 alone has just 5 scoreable rows — too few for a reliable MAE estimate.

Part B uses fold-3 training rows exclusively. Using any validation rows in Part B would constitute test leakage into the feature selection process.
