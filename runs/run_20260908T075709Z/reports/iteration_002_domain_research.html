Trigger 1 · Belgian Public Holidays
Belgium has 10 federal + 1 Flemish Community holiday = 11 official non-working days per year in Flanders. 8 are fixed calendar dates; 3 float with Easter (Easter Monday = Easter+1, Ascension = Easter+39, Whit Monday = Easter+50). Easter can shift by up to 5 weeks between years.

HIGH CONFIDENCE  Feature implication: compute floating holidays from Easter formula — never hardcode by month/day.
Trigger 2 · Gentse Feesten
Annual 10-day summer festival in central Ghent (~200,000 visitors/day, ~2 million total per edition). Runs from the Friday before Belgian National Day (July 21). 2020: CANCELLED due to COVID-19. Demand impact on branch 01 depends on its location relative to festival routes — unresolved without stakeholder confirmation (OQ-4).

MEDIUM CONFIDENCE  Feature implication: is_gentse_feesten must explicitly set 2020 = 0.
Trigger 3 · COVID-19 Lockdown (2020-03-18 to 2020-05-03)
Belgian bakeries were classified as essential food retail — permitted to remain OPEN throughout all lockdown phases. The 24 zero-demand days in 2020 are a mix of ~11 official holidays + voluntary closures. Expected demand during lockdown: ELEVATED (restaurant closures drove home cooking). An is_covid_lockdown_period binary covers the 47-day window.

HIGH CONFIDENCE
Trigger 4 · 2014 Sparse Data (64 / ~250 expected trading days)
Almost certainly caused by the POS system migration documented in storedata/Winkelfile.csv. The 64 days represent partial-year export from one side of the migration. Rolling statistics crossing 2014 are unreliable; lag-365 features from 2015 looking back to 2014 will find sparse data. Do not drop or impute — flag with is_2014_sparse_period.

HIGH CONFIDENCE

Key implementation constraint

Krokusvakantie and Paasvakantie float with Easter by up to 3 weeks. Any feature using fixed month-day windows (e.g. "Feb 15–22 = Krokus") will misclassify roughly 1–2 weeks per year. Easter formula computation is mandatory — not optional.

Anticipation effect

Day BEFORE a public holiday shows elevated bread demand in Belgian retail. Christmas Eve (Dec 24), Oct 31 (before All Saints'), and Good Friday are the strongest anticipation days. Feature: days_until_next_holiday (capped at 30).

COVID-19 Structural Shock

Belgian bakeries were permitted to stay open throughout all lockdown phases (essential food retail). Expected demand: ELEVATED — restaurant and cafe closures drove customers to cook at home, increasing bread consumption. Working from home increased weekday morning demand. Belgian supermarket scanner data confirms pantry-stocking in March 2020.

The 2020-06-01 regime change (shop went 7/7, opening Mondays) is almost certainly a COVID-driven decision: home-working customers created Monday demand that previously didn't exist (office workers formerly bought bread near their workplaces on weekdays).

Modelling caution

Rolling means that span March–May 2020 will be biased HIGH (lockdown-elevated demand). is_covid_lockdown_period and is_official_public_holiday are separate flags with different mechanisms — do not conflate them.

2014 POS Migration

Days recorded in 2014
64 / ~250
Only 26% of expected trading days; POS schema migration artifact

The sparse 2014 data (64 trading days vs ~250 expected) is a direct consequence of the POS system migration documented in storedata/Winkelfile.csv. The migration changed the XML schema (pre-migration: order/orderdetail → post-migration: sale/saledetail), and incomplete coverage for the migration year is a standard data transition artifact.

Rolling statistics (e.g. lag-365, annual seasonal components) that span 2014 will encounter this sparsity and produce incorrect values. The 64 days should remain in the series — flagged, not dropped.

Feature required:

is_2014_sparse_period — binary 1 for all 2014 dates. Allows tree-based models to learn a different intercept and prevents rolling stats from treating 2014 as a representative year.

Gentse Feesten 2020 — Unique Confound

July 2020 is the ONLY July in the training set without Gentse Feesten (festival cancelled due to COVID). July 2020 is simultaneously affected by: no festival, ongoing COVID behavioral changes, and the first weeks of the new Monday-trading regime. This makes July 2020 structurally unique and difficult for any model to predict correctly from prior Julys.

Competitor Closures (VerlofConcurrent)

REAL BUT SPARSE
Competitors tracked: 23
Coverage: 2017-07-01 to 2020-09-15
Gap at end: ~3.5 months

Genuine mechanism: when multiple competitors simultaneously close (primarily August summer vacation), a fraction of their customers redirect to open bakeries including Aernoudt branch 01. The redirection effect scales with the number of competitors simultaneously closed — a count/proportion is better than a binary flag.

Holiday confound

On public holidays, n_competitors_closed is high (most bakeries closed) AND Aernoudt demand may be zero. A naive correlation will be downward-biased. The genuine August vacation redirection signal is buried under this confound if not handled carefully.

Use competitor_closure_density (n / 23.0) with competitor_data_available indicator. VC2 is an exact duplicate of VC1 — do not use VC2.

Open Questions & Durable Rules
Stakeholder Questions & Binding Constraints

5 Open Questions for Stakeholder Resolution · 4 Durable Rules

Open Questions

  • HIGH OQ-1: Is the 2020-06-01 regime change to 7/7 permanent, or a temporary COVID measure? Was the shop open 7/7 before 2010? — if not permanent, regime_x_monday could harm holdout accuracy.
  • HIGH OQ-2: Does branch 01 close for an annual summer vacation (typically 1–3 weeks in August)? Fixed or variable dates? — a planned annual closure should be a predictable feature, not treated as demand suppression.
  • MED OQ-3: What products does bread_quantity capture exactly — all argcod=1 (278 catalog articles) or a subset? Specialty holiday breads included? — affects relevance of Sinterklaas, Easter, and Christmas period features.
  • MED OQ-4: Is branch 01 in or near central Ghent (within the Gentse Feesten festival footprint), or in a residential neighbourhood removed from festival routes? — determines whether is_gentse_feesten is low or high priority.
  • MED OQ-5: What explains 14 zero-demand trading days in 2019 (up from 3–6 in 2016–2018)? Policy change or anomaly? — may warrant an is_planned_closure feature if a closure policy changed.

Durable Rules (must not change)

  • Floating holidays must be computed, not hardcoded. Easter Monday, Ascension, Whit Monday, Krokusvakantie, Paasvakantie all move by up to 5 weeks between years. A fixed month-day approximation will be wrong for roughly half the training years, diluting the holiday signal.
  • Gentse Feesten 2020 must be explicitly set to 0 (cancelled). A generic "mid-July" date rule would set is_gentse_feesten=1 for 2020 — encoding elevated foot traffic that did not occur. This would corrupt the coefficient for all other years.
  • regime_x_monday cannot be replaced by day_of_week alone. All Monday training rows are post-2020-06-01. A plain DOW=Monday feature has no pre-regime Monday to anchor on; without the regime interaction, the model cannot distinguish regime-Monday from pre-regime Monday (which was a structural closure).
  • year_trend (continuous) is required; do NOT difference the target. The −28.7% decline is structural and trend-stationary (ADF p=0.0005). Differencing removes the most predictable trend component and prevents extrapolation of the decline into the 2021+ holdout.
